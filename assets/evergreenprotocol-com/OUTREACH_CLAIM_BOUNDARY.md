# Outreach Claim Boundary — evergreenprotocol.com

## Purpose

This document governs what may and may not be claimed in any operator outreach, engagement discussion, or strategic inquiry response on behalf of Evergreen Protocol. It applies to all communication formats: email, calls, memos, presentations, written proposals, and any other format used in operator engagement.

---

## Governing Principle

Claims made in operator engagement must be directly supportable by the public reference surface at evergreenprotocol.com. If a claim cannot be verified by a reader consulting the public site, it should not be made in outreach or operator discussion.

---

## Permitted Claims

### About the asset

- Evergreen Protocol is a citable category standard for reference continuity governance
- The category standard is published at version 1.0 and is publicly citable with citation formats
- The protocol is machine-readable through static, versioned JSON reference files at stable URLs
- The protocol applies to AI documentation, knowledge systems, digital infrastructure, and strategic digital assets, as documented in the reference application layer
- The readiness layer covers seven reference continuity dimensions as a self-orientation guide
- The public changelog documents the version history and governed review cadence
- The machine-readable reference files are static artifacts, not a live API or dynamic service

### About the operating model

- Qualified operators may discuss a non-exclusive operating pilot
- Pilot duration of 6 or 12 months may be discussed as a structure
- The evergreenprotocol.com domain and the category standard remain controlled by Sohadot during any pilot
- Operators may commercialize approved services built on the reference continuity framework under governed boundaries
- Revenue share from collected operating revenue may be discussed as part of a formal pilot arrangement
- Post-traction license or acquisition discussions may follow after demonstrated operating traction

### About the governance boundaries

- No certification is issued by Evergreen Protocol
- No compliance score, readiness score, or maturity score is produced
- No registry is open or operated
- No automatic acquisition right exists
- Public claims made by operators referencing the protocol require governance approval

---

## Prohibited Claims

### Prohibited asset claims

- That the protocol is a compliance tool, audit system, or certification body
- That the protocol has been adopted, endorsed, or validated by any external organization
- That the protocol carries regulatory authority or creates legal compliance status
- That the protocol is the exclusive or dominant standard in any market or sector
- Any claim about the asset's commercial value, purchase history, or comparable transactions
- Any claim not currently supported by the public reference surface

### Prohibited commercial claims

- Any specific price, percentage, or financial term in outreach or early-stage discussion (financial terms belong in formal written agreements only)
- That an operator has or will receive exclusive operating rights
- That an operator is the designated, preferred, or sole partner in any sector
- That operating engagement creates any automatic acquisition right
- That a pilot guarantees or creates a path to acquisition or institutional license
- That any timeline for acquisition or license is established or implied

### Prohibited certification and registry claims

- That the operator can certify organizations using the protocol
- That the operator can issue compliance status, ratings, scores, or assessment outcomes
- That the operator can maintain or reference a registry of assessed organizations
- That clients of the operator will receive any form of external recognition, badge, or designation referencing Evergreen Protocol

### Prohibited derivative claims

- That the operator can rename, rebrand, or create a derivative version of the protocol
- That the operator can alter the category standard, its scope, its governed terms, or its version history
- That the operator can publish the protocol or its reference artifacts under an alternative name or with modified content
- That the operator has any rights to the machine-readable reference files beyond consumption for service delivery purposes

---

## Language to Avoid

The following phrases create prohibited implications and must not appear in any operator communication:

- "certified by Evergreen Protocol"
- "Evergreen Protocol compliant" / "EP compliant"
- "registered under Evergreen Protocol"
- "validated by" / "endorsed by" / "recognized by" Evergreen Protocol
- "exclusive operator" / "preferred partner" / "authorized partner"
- "acquisition path confirmed" / "guaranteed licensing"
- "Evergreen Protocol-rated" / "Evergreen Protocol-scored" / "Evergreen Protocol-assessed"
- "our standard" when used by the operator to describe the protocol
- Any phrasing that implies the operator owns, co-owns, or controls the protocol

---

## When an Operator Asks About Terms Not in the Public Reference Surface

If an operator asks about terms, arrangements, or conditions not addressed in the public operator brief or strategic operating partnership page, the response is:

> "That would be addressed in a formal pilot structure discussion. The governance boundaries documented at `/operator-brief/` and `/strategic-operating-partnership/` apply to all operating discussions, and any additional terms would be confirmed in writing before any operating activity begins."

Do not improvise terms, make informal commitments, or create expectations in conversation that formal written documents do not support.
