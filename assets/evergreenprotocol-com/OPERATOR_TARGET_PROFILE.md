# Operator Target Profile — evergreenprotocol.com

## Purpose

This document defines the qualification criteria for identifying, assessing, and prioritizing potential operating partners for Evergreen Protocol. It governs the internal evaluation of operator fit before any outreach or engagement discussion is initiated.

---

## Primary Operator Types

The following five operator types represent the strongest alignment with Evergreen Protocol's category and governance model.

### 1. AI Governance Platforms

Platforms managing AI documentation, policy governance, and auditability across enterprise AI deployments. These operators face the reference continuity problem directly: their clients' AI systems generate and revise documentation at a rate that outpaces institutional governance capacity.

**Fit indicators:**
- Active enterprise AI governance product or service line
- Customers in regulated or compliance-sensitive environments
- Existing documentation, policy, or evidence governance workflow
- Interest in citable, versioned standards for AI-era governance positioning

### 2. GRC and Compliance Operators

Governance, risk, and compliance service providers whose clients face reference decay, evidence posture problems, and ungoverned documentation structures as AI accelerates institutional change.

**Fit indicators:**
- Active GRC advisory or platform offering
- Service lines covering documentation, policy, or knowledge governance
- Institutional or enterprise client base in regulated sectors
- Familiarity with reference standards and evidence auditability requirements

### 3. Enterprise Knowledge Governance Platforms

Platforms responsible for institutional knowledge architecture, controlled vocabulary, documentation governance, and definition management at scale.

**Fit indicators:**
- Enterprise knowledge management or governance product
- Controlled vocabulary, taxonomy, or definition management capability
- Version discipline or change governance features
- Institutional buyer focus with multi-department or enterprise-wide scope

### 4. Records Management and Digital Preservation Companies

Operators whose mandate includes long-horizon reference integrity, auditability of institutional records, and preservation of evidence posture under conditions of change.

**Fit indicators:**
- Records management, digital preservation, or archiving service line
- Institutional, government, or regulated-sector client base
- Understanding of citation stability and long-horizon auditability
- Experience with version control, document lifecycle governance, or archival standards

### 5. Institutional Advisory and Implementation Firms

Advisory and implementation organizations that help enterprises design, govern, and sustain reference-grade documentation and governance practices. These operators have client relationships and delivery capacity to commercialize reference continuity reviews and readiness workshops without requiring a platform.

**Fit indicators:**
- Advisory or implementation practice in knowledge governance, digital transformation, GRC, or institutional compliance
- Established enterprise or institutional client relationships
- Capacity to deliver structured reviews, assessments, or workshops
- Existing service lines into which reference continuity reviews fit naturally as a distinct deliverable

---

## Qualification Signals

A qualified operator exhibits the majority of the following:

- **Domain alignment** — their existing service line or platform directly addresses a problem adjacent to reference continuity
- **Institutional client base** — they serve enterprise, institutional, or regulated-sector customers
- **Governance sophistication** — they understand the distinction between a reference standard and a compliance tool
- **Boundary respect** — their operating model is unlikely to push toward certification, scoring, or registry behavior
- **Commercial capacity** — they have the operational capacity to develop and deliver services built on the protocol framework
- **Tone compatibility** — their public positioning is institutional, restrained, and non-gimmicky

---

## Disqualification Signals

The following signals indicate poor fit and should disqualify an operator from engagement:

- Primarily consumer-facing or mass-market product with no institutional service line
- History of certification badge programs, external scoring, or registry behavior not supported by a formal standard
- Promotional tone or branding incompatible with the asset's institutional positioning
- Interest in acquiring the domain for re-branding, sub-licensing, or resale without an operating intent
- Expectation of exclusive rights without demonstrated traction
- Assumption of an automatic acquisition path from pilot participation
- Request to alter the category standard, its scope, or its governed terms
- Inability to operate within governed claim boundaries in client-facing communications

---

## Fit Assessment Process

Before any engagement discussion is advanced:

1. Confirm the operator type aligns with one of the five primary types above
2. Verify that at least three qualification signals are present
3. Confirm that no disqualification signals are present
4. Review the operator's public-facing content for tone and positioning compatibility with the asset
5. Confirm that the domain-dossier engagement posture permits initiation of discussion

Fit assessment is an internal step. Operators are not informed of the assessment process or its outcome in early-stage communications.
