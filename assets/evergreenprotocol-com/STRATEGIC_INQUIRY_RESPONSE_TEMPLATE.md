# Strategic Inquiry Response Template — evergreenprotocol.com

## Purpose

This document provides governed templates for responding to inbound strategic inquiries about Evergreen Protocol. It covers the inquiry qualification process, response structure, and what to include and exclude in all responses.

This template applies to all inbound inquiries regardless of channel: email, referral, contact form, conference introduction, or other.

---

## Inquiry Qualification

Before responding, assess whether the inquiry warrants a substantive response.

**Respond with full template if:**
- The inquirer represents one of the five qualified operator types (AI governance platform, GRC operator, enterprise knowledge governance platform, records management or digital preservation company, institutional advisory or implementation firm)
- The inquiry demonstrates institutional intent — not curiosity, research, or domain brokerage interest
- The inquiry references a plausible service, platform, or advisory context
- The inquiry does not lead with a purchase offer, price question, or acquisition demand

**Respond with clarification template if:**
- The inquirer's institutional context is unclear or unstated
- The inquiry could represent qualified interest but lacks sufficient information to confirm fit
- The inquiry references a legitimate problem space but does not clearly identify the operator type

**Do not respond if:**
- The inquiry is from a domain broker, domain aggregator, or resale marketplace
- The inquiry leads with a purchase offer, price expectation, or broker representation
- The inquiry expects certification, registry access, or scoring behavior
- The inquiry is from a consumer-facing operator with no institutional service line
- The inquiry is speculative outreach with no identifiable institutional context

---

## Response Posture

All responses are:

- Institutional in tone — no promotional language, no urgency, no enthusiasm signaling
- Brief — two to four paragraphs in the initial response
- Non-committal on terms — no financial terms, no timeline, no eligibility confirmation in first contact
- Referential — direct the inquirer to the public governance documentation before advancing discussion

All responses avoid:
- Pricing or financial anchoring
- Urgency or scarcity framing
- Claims beyond what the public reference surface supports
- Promises about post-traction outcomes or acquisition paths
- Commitment to meetings, calls, or timelines in the first response

---

## Response Structure

**Paragraph 1 — Acknowledgment**
Acknowledge the inquiry briefly and without effusiveness. Note that Evergreen Protocol is a governed category asset with a structured operating discussion layer.

**Paragraph 2 — Reference**
Direct the inquirer to the two primary public governance documents. Ask them to review these before the discussion advances. Do not summarize the documents — let the documents speak.

**Paragraph 3 — Fit note (optional, for clearly qualified inquirers only)**
If the inquirer's stated context clearly places them in one of the five qualified operator types, briefly acknowledge that the operating layer is designed for operators in their category. Do not confirm eligibility or express enthusiasm. Keep this paragraph to one or two sentences.

**Paragraph 4 — Next step**
State what a productive next response would contain: the inquirer's service line context, their client base, and the type of engagement they are considering. Do not propose a meeting or call in the first response.

## Template A — Standard Inbound Inquiry (Qualified Institutional Context)

---

Thank you for reaching out regarding Evergreen Protocol.

Evergreen Protocol is a governed category standard and machine-readable reference layer for AI-era digital continuity. Operating discussions are structured through a controlled layer designed for qualified institutional operators, not a public partner program or open application process.

Before advancing any discussion, I would ask you to review two documents:

- Operator Brief: https://evergreenprotocol.com/operator-brief/
- Strategic Operating Partnership: https://evergreenprotocol.com/strategic-operating-partnership/

These cover the commercial logic, operating model, governance boundaries, and suggested pilot structure. They also make clear what this asset is not: there is no certification program, no registry, and no automatic acquisition path.

If the operating model is relevant to your context after reviewing those documents, please reply with specific information about your organization's service line, your institutional client base, and the type of engagement you are considering. That context will allow for a more substantive response.

---

Thank you for your message.

Before responding further, could you share more about your organization and the nature of your interest in Evergreen Protocol? Specifically:

- What type of service line or platform does your organization operate?
- Who are your primary institutional clients?
- What kind of operating or licensing arrangement are you considering?

This context will help determine whether a substantive discussion is appropriate.

In the meantime, the public governance documentation is available at:

- https://evergreenprotocol.com/operator-brief/
- https://evergreenprotocol.com/strategic-operating-partnership/

## Template C — Non-Qualified Inquiry (Domain Brokerage or Resale Interest)

For inquiries from domain brokers, resale interests, or parties with no institutional operating context, no substantive response is required. If any response is sent:

Thank you for your message. Evergreen Protocol is not available through domain brokers or resale channels. The asset is governed through a structured operating discussion layer for qualified institutional operators. No further information is available through this channel.

## Template D — Unsolicited Acquisition Offer

For unsolicited acquisition offers received before the asset's engagement posture has shifted, no response is required. If any response is sent:

Any post-traction acquisition discussion would follow a demonstrated operating relationship, not an unsolicited offer. The asset is not available for direct domain sale. No further response will be provided on this channel.

## Post-Response Process

After sending any response to a qualified or potentially qualified inquirer:

1. Log the inquiry date, channel, inquirer context, and response sent (maintain an internal engagement log)
2. If the inquirer responds with qualifying context, assess against `OPERATOR_TARGET_PROFILE.md` before advancing
3. If fit is confirmed, advance discussion to pilot structure using `PILOT_STRUCTURE_DRAFT.md` as the internal reference
4. Maintain claim discipline throughout all follow-up communications using `OUTREACH_CLAIM_BOUNDARY.md`
5. Do not advance to formal pilot discussion without confirming the domain-dossier engagement posture permits it
6. No financial terms, no acquisition terms, and no exclusivity terms are discussed before the formal pilot agreement is in preparation

---

## What Not to Do in Any Response

- Do not confirm that the inquirer is eligible or qualified
- Do not express enthusiasm, urgency, or flattery
- Do not volunteer information about other interested parties
- Do not describe internal documents or internal processes
- Do not suggest a timeline for any agreement
- Do not discuss price, revenue share percentages, or acquisition valuation
- Do not make claims not present in the public reference surface
- Do not agree to a call or meeting in the first response — require the inquirer to demonstrate engagement with the governance documentation first
