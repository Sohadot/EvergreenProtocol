# Operator Outreach Brief — evergreenprotocol.com

## Purpose

This document is the internal reference for preparing and conducting operator outreach when engagement becomes appropriate. It governs posture, framing, content, and conduct for any direct contact with qualified operators.

This document does not authorize outreach. The domain-dossier engagement posture governs when outreach is appropriate.

---

## Current Engagement Posture

**Hold.**

As of the current maturity stage (positioned), no outreach should be initiated. The strongest next move is continued authority construction, not buyer-facing motion. This document exists to govern outreach when the posture shifts, not to accelerate it.

Posture changes require a dossier update and should follow Gate 5 (Anti-Mobility Discipline).

---

## What Constitutes a Qualified Outreach Context

Outreach becomes appropriate when:

- The asset has accumulated visible authority and structural evidence sufficient to carry part of its own strategic case
- The public reference surface is complete enough that an operator can orient without extended explanation
- At least one inbound inquiry from a qualified operator type has been received and assessed
- The domain-dossier engagement posture has been explicitly updated from "Do not engage yet"

---

## What to Lead With

When outreach is appropriate, the communication leads with the asset's institutional function, not its sale status:

- The category standard and its governed scope
- The machine-readable reference layer and its institutional applications
- The reference application domains
- The operator brief as the primary entry point for discussion
- The pilot structure as a framework for any operating arrangement

Do not lead with domain value, acquisition framing, or pricing.

---

## Permitted Outreach Formats

- Restrained executive email (one to three paragraphs, referencing the public operator brief)
- Strategic inquiry memo (one to two pages, built from the public reference surface)
- Direct referral through a governed operating relationship

## Prohibited Outreach Formats

- Mass email or automated outreach sequence
- Broker-mediated domain sale outreach
- Cold social media message
- Public marketplace listing
- Press release, announcement, or promotional content framing the asset as available for sale

---

## What to Include in Outreach

Include:
- Brief framing of the category problem (reference continuity under AI-era change)
- Reference to the public operator brief at `/operator-brief/`
- Reference to the strategic operating partnership layer at `/strategic-operating-partnership/`
- An invitation to review the governance documentation before advancing any discussion

Do not include:
- Pricing or financial anchoring of any kind
- Urgency framing or scarcity language
- Acquisition pressure or timeline
- Claims not present in the public reference surface
- Competitor comparisons or market-share framing

---

## Tone

Institutional. Restrained. Non-promotional. Conceptually clear.

Outreach tone must match the tone of the public reference surface. Outreach that is louder, more promotional, or more urgent than the asset's own pages damages the asset's positioning and lowers the perceived seriousness of the operating discussion.
