Strategic Operating Partnership

Evergreen Protocol is available for qualified strategic operating partnerships, institutional licensing, and post-traction acquisition discussions with operators capable of commercializing AI-era digital continuity without compromising the protocol's reference integrity.

What the Asset Provides

  • Citable category standard - a public reference for reference continuity governance.
  • Category boundary - explicit scope and non-claim boundaries for adjacent governance domains.
  • Versioned machine-readable reference layer - static JSON files for definitions, citation, and standard summary.
  • Reference applications - controlled application pages across AI documentation, knowledge systems, digital infrastructure, and strategic digital assets.
  • Reference continuity readiness entry point - a practical self-orientation guide for institutional readers.
  • Public changelog - visible version history and review cadence.
  • Citation guidance - formats for referencing the standard and its governed terms.
  • Governed claim boundaries - limits that preserve reference integrity as operating models are explored.

Qualified Operating Partners

Potential operating partnerships may be discussed with qualified operators whose existing work aligns with institutional reference continuity and who can preserve the standard's governed boundaries.

  • AI governance platforms
  • GRC and compliance operators
  • Enterprise knowledge governance companies
  • Digital preservation and records management operators
  • Institutional advisory or implementation firms

Operating Models

The following models may be discussed with qualified operators, subject to governance approval and preservation of the category standard:

  • Non-exclusive operating pilot
  • Institutional license
  • Sector-specific operating rights
  • Reference review delivery partnership
  • Post-traction acquisition discussion after demonstrated traction

What May Be Commercialized

Qualified operators may discuss delivery models built around reference continuity work, including:

  • Reference Continuity Review
  • AI Documentation Continuity Review
  • Knowledge System Continuity Review
  • Protocol Continuity Brief
  • Enterprise readiness workshop
  • Internal documentation continuity review

These are potential operating models, not currently issued certifications or public compliance statuses.

Boundaries

  • The protocol is not a certification system.
  • The readiness layer does not produce scores.
  • Reference applications do not create compliance status.
  • Machine-readable files are static reference artifacts, not a live API.
  • No registry is currently open.
  • Operating partners may not alter the category standard or create uncontrolled derivative claims.

Future Acquisition Path

A successful operating partnership may create a basis for later acquisition discussions. No automatic acquisition right is granted by publication of this page.
