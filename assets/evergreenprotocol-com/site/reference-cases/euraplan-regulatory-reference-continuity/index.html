Self-applied reference case · regulatory reference continuity

EuraPlan — Regulatory Reference Continuity

This case documents how Evergreen Protocol principles have been applied to govern citation stability, update discipline, and the reference-versus-advice boundary within a multi-country regulatory reference structure under ongoing legislative change.

Case Type

Regulatory reference continuity. A multi-country regulatory reference structure where the underlying legislative content changes continuously, but the reference surface must remain citable, bounded, and institutionally readable across review cycles.

Continuity Problem

Regulatory reference surfaces face a continuity challenge distinct from static knowledge assets: the subject matter they document changes continuously through legislative amendment, transposition into national law, guidance revision, and superseding instruments. This creates a set of compounding problems:

  • Citation against a moving target — when a reader cites a regulation entry, the regulation itself may have been amended; a reference surface without version-dated citations cannot be relied upon as a stable citation anchor.
  • Country-regulation structure drift — a multi-country regulatory surface must maintain a consistent organizational structure even as individual country implementations diverge; without explicit structure governance, cross-country comparisons lose coherence.
  • Update trigger ambiguity — calendar-based review cycles are misaligned with regulatory change; entries need review when the underlying regulation changes, not when an arbitrary interval expires.
  • Reference-versus-advice boundary erosion — a regulatory reference surface that expands toward interpretation, recommendation, or compliance guidance crosses from reference into advice, creating both legal and epistemic problems.
  • Long-horizon institutional readability — a reader returning to the surface after a period of regulatory change must be able to determine what the surface was claiming at the time of a prior citation, not just what it claims now.

Evergreen Protocol Principles Demonstrated

  • Bounded scope — the reference surface covers regulatory information and explicitly excludes legal advice, compliance assessment, interpretation, or recommendation. The boundary is stated in the surface itself, not assumed by readers.
  • Version-dated citations — each regulation entry includes the version or date of the instrument being referenced; citations remain interpretable even after the underlying regulation is amended, because readers can identify which version was documented.
  • Event-triggered review cadence — entries carry explicit next-review triggers tied to legislative events (amendment publication, transposition deadline, guidance revision) rather than calendar intervals; this aligns update discipline with the actual change rate of the subject matter.
  • Structure governance — the country-and-regulation organizational structure is a governed artifact; changes to the structure itself follow a documented process separate from changes to individual entries.
  • Temporal validity marking — entries record the period during which their content was accurate; later readers can determine the temporal validity of a prior citation rather than assuming the current entry reflects the state at the time of citation.
  • Continuity boundary maintenance — the advice boundary is restated at each point where the surface could drift toward interpretation; this is a structural governance commitment, not an editorial judgment made entry by entry.

What This Case Does Not Claim

  • This case does not certify EuraPlan as legally compliant with any regulatory requirement.
  • This case does not validate the legal accuracy or completeness of any regulation entry.
  • This case does not assess the comprehensiveness or authority of the regulatory reference surface relative to other sources.
  • This case does not establish Evergreen Protocol as a legal reference standard or as an authority over regulatory documentation.
  • This case does not create any compliance status, approval, or external recognition for the asset.
