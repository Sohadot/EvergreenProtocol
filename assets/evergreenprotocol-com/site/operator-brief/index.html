Operator Brief

This brief is for qualified enterprise operators in AI governance, GRC, enterprise knowledge governance, digital preservation, records management, and institutional advisory who are considering strategic operating engagement with Evergreen Protocol.

Executive Summary

Evergreen Protocol is a citable category standard and machine-readable reference layer for AI-era digital continuity, designed to help institutions preserve reference integrity, evidence posture, version discipline, and knowledge continuity under accelerated change.

It is a governed category asset — not a SaaS product, not a compliance registry, and not a certification body. Its value lies in its function as a stable, citable reference layer that qualified operators can build institutional services around under governed boundaries.

The Operating Problem

AI-era documentation changes faster than institutional governance can stabilize it. The consequences compound:

  • Policies and references decay between review cycles.
  • Evidence posture becomes unclear as AI-generated outputs replace stable documentation.
  • Versions become difficult to cite when the underlying content is continuously updated without version discipline.
  • Institutional knowledge loses auditability when there is no governed reference layer to anchor decisions.
  • Organizations that depend on stable references for governance, compliance, or advisory work find themselves operating on documentation that cannot be verified, cited, or traced.

The existing governance frameworks address compliance, security, and risk. The specific problem of reference continuity — keeping knowledge, evidence, and protocols valid, versioned, citable, and operationally coherent over time — remains under-governed. Organizations need governed reference continuity.

What Evergreen Protocol Provides

  • Category standard — a publicly citable declaration of the reference continuity governance category, at version 1.0.
  • Category boundary — explicit scope and non-claim limits that distinguish reference continuity from adjacent governance domains.
  • Citable version protocol — a versioned, documented governance unit with bounded scope, named roles, required artifacts, and a defined review cadence.
  • Machine-readable reference artifacts — static versioned JSON files for definitions, citation formats, and standard summary, consumable by AI agents and institutional systems.
  • Reference applications — controlled public application pages across AI documentation, knowledge systems, digital infrastructure, and strategic digital assets.
  • Readiness entry point — a self-orientation guide for institutional readers across seven reference continuity dimensions.
  • Public changelog — a visible version history and governed review cadence, self-applied to the standard itself.
  • Strategic operating partnership layer — a governed surface for qualified operating, licensing, and post-traction acquisition discussions.

Who This Is For

This brief is directed at qualified institutional operators whose existing service lines, platforms, or advisory practices align with reference continuity governance. Relevant operator types include:

  • AI governance platforms — platforms managing AI documentation, policy governance, and auditability across enterprise AI deployments.
  • GRC and compliance operators — governance, risk, and compliance service providers whose clients face reference decay and evidence posture problems as AI accelerates documentation change.
  • Enterprise knowledge governance platforms — platforms responsible for institutional knowledge architecture, controlled vocabulary, and documentation governance at scale.
  • Records management and digital preservation companies — operators whose mandate includes long-horizon reference integrity and auditability of institutional records.
  • Institutional advisory and implementation firms — advisory and implementation organizations that help enterprises design, govern, and sustain reference-grade documentation practices.

Suggested Pilot Structure

Qualified operators may discuss a structured pilot arrangement with the following characteristics:

  • Duration — 6-month or 12-month non-exclusive operating pilot.
  • Domain and protocol control — the evergreenprotocol.com domain and the Evergreen Protocol category standard remain controlled by Sohadot throughout the pilot period.
  • Commercialization scope — the operator may commercialize approved services built around the reference continuity framework under governed boundaries.
  • Revenue share — revenue share arrangements from collected operating revenue may be discussed as part of the pilot structure.
  • Public claims governance — all public claims referencing the protocol remain subject to governance approval; no uncontrolled derivative standard or uncontrolled claim is permitted.
  • Post-traction path — post-traction institutional license or acquisition discussions may follow after demonstrated operating traction.

No automatic acquisition right is granted by this brief or by entering a pilot arrangement.

Governance Boundaries

The following constraints apply to any operating engagement with Evergreen Protocol:

  • No certification program. Evergreen Protocol does not certify organizations, individuals, or systems.
  • No score. No readiness score, compliance score, or maturity score is produced or issued.
  • No registry. No public or private registry of certified or assessed organizations is opened.
  • No external compliance status. Reference continuity is a governed framework layer, not an externally-issued compliance designation.
  • No uncontrolled derivative standard. Operators may not create derivative versions of the category standard, alter its scope, or publish it under an alternative name without governance approval.
  • No automatic acquisition right. Operating engagement, pilot completion, and revenue generation do not confer any automatic right to acquire the domain or the protocol.
  • No alteration of category standard without governance approval. The category standard, its boundary, and its version history are controlled by Sohadot.

Why Now

The case for governed reference continuity is strongest in periods of rapid documentation change. Several conditions have converged:

  • AI systems have materially increased the speed of documentation and workflow change across enterprises, regulatory environments, and institutional governance layers.
  • Enterprises that depend on stable references for compliance, audit, advisory, and governance functions are encountering reference decay at rates that existing document management practices were not designed to handle.
  • Machine-readable standards are becoming a baseline expectation for AI-era governance infrastructure — organizations need reference layers that AI agents can consume, cite, and verify.
  • Governance without reference continuity becomes fragile: decisions made on decayed references, uncitable policies, and ungoverned documentation create compounding institutional risk.

The category standard is published, versioned, and machine-readable. The operating window for qualified operators is open before the category becomes contested.

Strategic Fit

Evergreen Protocol is designed to fit inside existing institutional service lines without requiring those service lines to be restructured around it. Integration points include:

  • AI governance workflows — the protocol's reference continuity standard provides a stable anchor for AI documentation, evidence posture, and version discipline inside AI governance programs.
  • GRC service lines — reference continuity reviews and AI documentation continuity reviews integrate naturally into GRC delivery models as a distinct and increasingly necessary governance layer.
  • Knowledge governance platforms — the protocol's controlled vocabulary, definition governance, and machine-readable reference artifacts fit inside platform-level knowledge governance infrastructure.
  • Documentation governance — institutional documentation governance programs can adopt the protocol's versioning, changelog, and citation discipline as a reference-grade standard layer.
  • Digital preservation and records management — the protocol's emphasis on version discipline, auditability, and long-horizon reference integrity aligns directly with the mandate of records management and digital preservation operators.
  • Institutional advisory offerings — advisory firms can incorporate reference continuity reviews and readiness workshops as distinct, citable advisory deliverables supported by the protocol's public reference layer.
