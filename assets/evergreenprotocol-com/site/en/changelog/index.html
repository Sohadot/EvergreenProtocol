Version governance · self-applied

Changelog

Evergreen Protocol applies its own standard to itself. This changelog is the version record of the standard — governed by the same review cadence, authority structure, and change discipline the standard defines for all reference systems.

Change governance

Changes to Evergreen Protocol are governed by the following rules, which mirror the Methodology:

  • Major changes — material revisions to the category definition, scope boundary, or core definitions — require a formal review cycle and produce a new major version.
  • Minor changes — clarifications, additions, or elaborations that do not alter the category boundary — produce a minor version update.
  • Patch changes — typographical, navigational, or presentational corrections — are recorded but do not change the version number.
  • Review trigger — a review may be triggered by a material change in the regulatory landscape, a significant shift in AI governance standards, or a formal request from an institutional user of the standard.

Review cadence

Evergreen Protocol undergoes a formal validity review on an annual cycle, aligned with the Zero-Season Algorithm principle: review is tied to validity and dependency change, not arbitrary scheduling.

Between formal reviews, the standard is considered active and citable at its current version. Any interim changes are recorded here with the rationale and governing authority.
